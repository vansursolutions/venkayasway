**Full description (4000 chars max):**
Official app of Sri Venkaiah Swamy Temple, Sullurupeta, Andhra Pradesh.

Bhagavan Sri Venkaiah Swamy, the Avadhuta saint of Golagamudi, is revered by devotees as an incarnation of Lord Dattatreya. This app brings the temple to your phone, in English and Telugu.

• Life story and teachings of Swamy
• Darshan timings, daily poojas and festival dates
• Upcoming events and the Saturday annadanam schedule
• Register to sponsor a Saturday annadanam in your family's name
• Photo and video gallery, and the temple's YouTube channel
• Directions to the temple, contact and donation details

Om Narayana Adi Narayana.
